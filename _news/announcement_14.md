Our abstract [**Spike-Coding Networks Are Exactly Solvable: Periodic Firing Patterns as Eigenvectors**](https://snufa.net/2026/abstracts/abel-sagodi-spike-coding.html) will be presented as a poster at the [SNUFA 2026](https://snufa.net/2026/) online workshop (4-5 November). This is joint work with Bill Podlaski and Ildefonso Ferreira Pica. See [this figure](https://github.com/Asagodi/scn2ppm/blob/main/docs/img/scn_word_example_paper.png) for an overview of the results, and the [code on GitHub](https://github.com/Asagodi/scn2ppm).
