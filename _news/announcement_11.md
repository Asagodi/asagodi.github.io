Two posters were accepted for [Dynamics Days Europe](https://dde-2026.sci-meet.net/)! The first one is **Universal Approximation Theorems for Dynamical Systems with Infinite-Time Horizon Guarantees in collaboration** with Memming Park, while the second is **Piecewise Projective Maps for Analytical Solutions of Spiking Neural Network Dynamics** with Ildefonso Ferreira Pica and Bill Podlaski.
